## Portfolio production and application writing

This page discloses how the research portfolio was produced. It is not a certification that AI-generated passages may be submitted as original application writing.

[Common App's fraud policy](https://www.commonapp.org/fraud-policy/) addresses misrepresentation of AI-generated substantive content as an applicant's own original work. [Georgia Tech's guidance](https://admission.gatech.edu/ai) allows thoughtful brainstorming and editing support while requiring the final writing submission to be the applicant's own.

A portfolio disclosure does not substitute for those requirements. Application descriptions should truthfully represent the research contribution and assistance received, and the submitted wording must satisfy the relevant institution's rules. Authorship claims, permission to display team work and compliance with application-writing requirements are separate questions.
